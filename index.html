What to check before approving shipment

  1. Save the exact listing and variant. Record the size, color and seller link so you can match them against your order.
  2. Compare measurements. For shoes, compare insole length if available. For a hoodie or jacket, measure the chest width and body length of a garment you already wear.
  3. Review warehouse QC photos if available. Check the item and size label against your order. For shoes, ask for both soles if the standard photos omit them. For clothing, ask for a clear photo of any seam or print you want to inspect.
  4. Check the exchange terms before shipping internationally. Confirm the return window and any local return postage with your agent while the parcel is still at the warehouse.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product comparison list for shoppers who use a buying agent. This guide uses selected CAFANS listings and lets you export filtered results as a CSV for Excel or Google Sheets.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The displayed figure is a CAFANS item-price estimate. Get a destination-specific shipping quote and confirm fees, taxes and duties before setting your total budget.

Where do I place an order?

Open the product on CAFANS, review the details, and check the available seller or agent links for that listing. Confirm the exact variant and final cost with the service handling your order.
